🥗 Healthy vs 🍔 Junk Food

Learn the difference between healthy and junk foods and make better food choices every day.

🍎

Apple

Healthy

Rich in fiber, vitamins and antioxidants.

Calories: 52 kcal

Fiber: 2.4 g

Sugar: 10 g

🥗

Vegetable Salad

Healthy

A nutritious combination of fresh vegetables.

Calories: 80 kcal

Fiber: 4 g

Protein: 3 g

🥣

Oats

Healthy

Whole grain food containing fiber and nutrients.

Calories: 389 kcal / 100g

Fiber: 10.6 g

Protein: 16.9 g

🍔

Burger

Junk

Often high in calories, sodium and saturated fat.

Calories: ~300-600 kcal

Fat: High

Sodium: High

🍕

Pizza

Junk

Can be high in refined carbohydrates, sodium and fat.

Calories: ~250-350 kcal / slice

Fat: Moderate-High

Sodium: High

🍟

French Fries

Junk

Fried food that can be high in calories and fat.

Calories: ~312 kcal / 100g

Fat: ~15 g

Fiber: ~3.8 g

🥤

Soft Drink

Junk

Sugary drinks can add a large amount of added sugar.

Calories: ~42 kcal / 100ml

Sugar: ~10.6 g / 100ml

Fiber: 0 g

🥗 Healthy Food vs 🍔 Junk Food

Healthy Food Junk Food
Usually nutrient-rich Often nutrient-poor
Usually contains more fiber Often contains less fiber
Less processed options Often highly processed
Supports a balanced diet Best consumed occasionally
Fruits, vegetables, whole grains Fries, sugary drinks, processed snacks

💡 Healthy Eating Tips

🥦 Add more vegetables to your meals.

🍎 Choose whole fruits instead of sugary snacks.

💧 Drink enough water throughout the day.

🥜 Include nutritious protein and healthy-fat sources.

🍔 Keep highly processed foods as occasional choices.
